## 1. Question

Is the concentration of P&L in the single dominant "opportunity window"
(window 19 in the train+cv audit of the 28-trade regime-confidence-gated
ETH forecaster) driven by a **feature-detectable recurring market state**,
or is it a one-shot lucky episode with no distinguishable fingerprint?

This test is the gate Codex imposed before any convexity-infrastructure
build. A pass means the motif that produced window 19 recurs and is
pre-identifiable from features available at the entry point. A fail
means window 19 is an anomaly and the regime-gated tail-capture strategy
class is abandoned entirely (or treated as speculative lottery
allocation only, not infrastructure investment).

`autoresearch/forecasters/regime_confidence_gated.py` wrapped around

The base forecaster is fit on TRAIN (first 60% of manifest) with

across train+cv (holdout excluded).

## 5. Window definition (frozen)

```yaml
window_identification:
  gap_threshold_markets: 10
  min_window_size: 1
```

A window is a run of market indices where the gated forecaster
fires on at least one market, with at most 10 non-firing markets
between consecutive firings. This matches the 2026-04-08 audit.

## 7. Cohorts

**Positive cohort (target)**: the 11 trade entry states that make up
window 19 in the audit run. Window 19 is identified by its start and
end market indices in the audit log; those exact indices are the
authoritative definition.

**Negative cohort A - non-winning opportunity windows**: entry states
of trades in the other 24 windows identified by the same audit run,
excluding the windows classified as "winners" per the criterion below.

**Negative cohort B - random non-firing markets**: a random sample of
500 markets from the train+cv partition where the gated forecaster
does NOT fire (all snapshots return NaN). Sampled at the market level
with numpy random seed 20260408.

**"Winning window" criterion**: a window whose total log growth across
all trades within it is >= +0.05 (compounded bankroll multiplier >=
1.05x across that window's trades). Per the audit, there are 2
windows meeting this criterion: window 19 (+0.4137) and window 1
(+0.1206). Window 19 is the positive cohort. Window 1 is the
**held-out winning window** used for the recurrence test (step 11.2).
All other non-window-19 windows are negative cohort A.

## 8. Classifier

Simple kNN classifier with `k = 5`, Euclidean distance in
z-score-normalised feature space. Normalisation fit on the union of
all positive + negative cohort samples, applied uniformly.

Why kNN: simplest possible discriminator, no assumption of parametric
form, no hyperparameter search beyond k (fixed at 5 by spec rule to
avoid post-hoc tuning), interpretable as "is this state near states
that were profitable."

## 9. Evaluation: leave-one-window-out

Train set: all 11 window-19 entries as positive + all 500
non-firing random market samples as negative.

Test protocol:

1. Fit the kNN classifier on the training set.
2. For each of the 11 window-19 entries, compute leave-one-out
   prediction: fit kNN excluding that entry, predict on it, record
   class probability of positive class.
3. For each entry state in negative cohort A (non-winning windows),
   predict class probability.
4. For each market in a 500-sample held-out non-firing set (distinct
   from training negative cohort), predict class probability.
5. Compute AUC of positive-class probability for distinguishing:
   - Positive (held-out window-19 entries) vs Held-out random negatives
   - Positive vs Negative cohort A

6. **Recurrence check**: compute class probability for each entry
   in window 1 (the held-out winning window). If the mean class
   probability of window 1 entries is materially higher than the
   mean for negative cohort A, window-19 features are associated
   with profitability - the motif recurred on at least one
   independent profitable window.

## 10. Pre-registered pass/fail thresholds

The test PASSES only if ALL of:

1. **AUC vs random non-firing markets** (LOOCV on positive cohort +
   500 held-out random negatives): >= 0.70

2. **AUC vs non-winning opportunity windows** (positive cohort vs
   negative cohort A): >= 0.65

3. **Recurrence on window 1**: mean class probability for window 1
   entries is >= the 75th percentile of class probabilities for
   negative cohort A

If ANY of these fail, the test FAILS and the tail-capture regime-gated
hypothesis is abandoned as a framework infrastructure investment (it
can still be used for speculative small-allocation lottery bets if
desired, but no further infrastructure will be built for it).

## 11. What a pass does NOT mean

Even if this test passes, the strategy is not yet validated for
production or even for holdout testing. A pass only means "the
motif is detectable in the training+CV data and recurs on at least
one additional profitable window, so it's worth building narrow
convexity validation infrastructure to test more rigorously." The
holdout partition (1112 markets) remains untouched and can only be
burned via a separate, later preregistration once the convexity
validation module exists.
